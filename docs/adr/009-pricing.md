# ADR-009: Pricing model

- Status: Accepted (see `docs/business/offer.md`)
- Decision: one plan, **Bs 350/location/month** (≈ USD 50), annual **Bs 3,500**, no contract, no termination fee, free onboarding, 30-day guarantee, founder price locked for life for the first 20 locations. No hardware subsidy and no payment take-rate in 2026.
- Guardrail: the per-location SIN provider cost must stay ≤ 20% of price. Otherwise it's passed through openly as a separate line.
- Review: Q2 2027 (tiers, re-pricing new clients for FX).
