# ADR-009: Pricing model

- Status: **Revised** (2026-10-04, see `docs/business/offer.md`)
- Decision:
  - One plan: **Bs 350/location/month**, annual **Bs 3,500**. Priced in Bs, not publicly pegged to USD.
  - No contract and no termination fee. Free setup for founders.
  - First month paid on installation day, with a full refund if cancelled within 30 days.
  - Founders (first 20 locations): Bs 350 locked for 24 months, then 30% off the list price for life.
  - Includes 2,000 SIN invoices/month per location (provisional); above that, billed at provider cost.
  - Accountant referral: 20% of the client's payments for 12 months.
  - No hardware subsidy and no payment take-rate in 2026.
- Guardrail: the SIN provider's cost per location must stay ≤ 20% of price at typical volume.
- Review: after the first 10 price-question answers, and in Q2 2027 (tiers, re-pricing new clients for FX).
