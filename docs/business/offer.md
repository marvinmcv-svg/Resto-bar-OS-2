# The Offer: "Plan Fundador"

> Goal: 20 paying restaurants/bars by **Dec 31, 2026**, at ~USD 50/month priced in **bolivianos**.
> Anything marked `[VERIFY]` is an assumption to confirm before it goes into sales material.
> Revised 2026-10-04 after critical review: safer terms, honest promises, lower hardware barrier.

## 1. One plan, one price

| | Plan Fundador |
|---|---|
| List price | **Bs 350 / month** per location. Priced in Bs on value; **not publicly pegged to USD** (FX is volatile) |
| Annual prepay | **Bs 3,500 / year** (pay 10 months, get 12) |
| Founder lock | First 20 locations: **Bs 350 locked for 24 months**, then **30% off the list price for life** |
| Contract | None. Month-to-month, cancel anytime, **no termination fee** |
| Setup | **Free for founders, in person**: menu load, printer setup, staff training (1 visit) |
| Payment start | **First month paid on installation day** (QR/transfer) |
| Guarantee | **Cancel within 30 days → full refund of the first month.** No questions asked |
| Invoices included | Up to **2,000 SIN invoices/month** per location (provisional, `[VERIFY]` against provider cost). Above that, billed at the provider's cost, shown on our own invoice |
| Payment methods | QR (bank QR), bank transfer; annual can also be paid in USD/USDT |

Why these terms:
- **Paid at install + 30-day refund** instead of "you don't pay if it doesn't fit". Installs are our scarcest resource (4-5/week). Asking for payment filters out curious owners without adding risk for serious ones.
- **24-month lock + 30% off for life** instead of "price locked for life". A Bs price frozen forever can lose half its value in an unstable FX market. Founders still get a deal that is clearly better than anyone after them.
- **Invoice allowance** instead of a vague "pass-through". "Everything included" stays true for almost every client, and the rare high-volume bar knows the rule upfront.

Rules:
- Re-price the Bs list price for **new** clients every 12 months.
- Push annual prepay hardest in November-December: cash before peak season, and no FX risk.
- Extra locations: same price. Extra devices: free.
- **Price test:** ask the price question in the first 10 conversations (see §5). Change the price only if more than 5 of 10 owners say no because of price, not because of trust or features.

## 4. Hardware: the hidden barrier
The hardware can cost more upfront than a year of the subscription, so we keep it minimal and tell the owner the exact cost **before** the visit.
- **Minimum kit:** 1 network thermal printer (kitchen) + 1 Android device for the cashier (tablet or phone they already own).
- **Optional:** a 2nd printer for the bar; a tablet for the kitchen screen.
- **Waiters:** their own phones, Android or iPhone, in the browser (staff PIN; nothing installed, no personal data stored).
- **Backup internet:** the owner's phone hotspot or a prepaid 4G modem (§6).
- We don't resell hardware in 2026. We send a shopping list with local prices `[VERIFY in Santa Cruz]`.

## 5. Objection handling

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Ya tengo sistema." | "Does it send you the close on WhatsApp and stop waiters from deleting sales? Try it 30 days: if you cancel, we refund you." |
| "Es caro." | "It's less than one drink per day. If it doesn't pay for itself in 30 days, we give you your money back." |

| "Me da miedo cambiar." | "We install it and train your staff ourselves. If anything fails, you print a paper order pad from the system and keep working." |

| "Tengo que comprar tablets." | "No. Waiters use their own phones. You only need one printer for the kitchen." |
| "¿Y si ustedes desaparecen?" | "You can export your data anytime, and there's no contract." |
| "Necesito pensarlo." | "Of course. There are 20 founder places; N are left." (Keep the counter real.) |

**Price question (first 10 conversations):** "If this cost Bs 350 per month, would it be cheap, fair, expensive, or too expensive for your place?" Log the answer in the scoreboard.

## 6. Spanish one-pager / WhatsApp script

**Mensaje inicial (warm lead / referido):**
> Hola [Nombre], soy Marvin. Estoy lanzando un sistema para restaurantes y bares de Santa Cruz: tus meseros toman pedidos desde su celular, la comanda sale en cocina, la factura sale de la misma cuenta y te llega el cierre del día a tu WhatsApp. Sin contrato, Bs 350 al mes, instalación gratis. Busco a los primeros 20 locales con precio fundador. ¿Te muestro 15 minutos en tu local esta semana?

**Guion de demo (15 minutos):**
1. (2 min) Pregunta: ¿cómo toman pedidos hoy? ¿cómo facturan? ¿cómo saben cuánto vendieron anoche?
2. (5 min) Pedido en mesa desde un celular → comanda en cocina → dividir la cuenta → cobro con QR → factura.
3. (2 min) Intenta borrar un cobro con el PIN de mesero: el sistema no lo permite.
4. (3 min) **Envía el reporte de cierre a su WhatsApp, ahí mismo.**
5. (3 min) Oferta: precio fundador, instalación gratis, pagas el primer mes el día de instalación, y si en 30 días no te sirve, te devolvemos tu dinero. "¿Lo instalamos el martes o el jueves?"

**Cierre:** always propose a concrete installation date. Never end with "te mando la información".

**Backup if the internet fails during service (onboarding checklist):** the router is set to fail over to the owner's hotspot or 4G modem. If everything fails, print the paper order pad from the system and enter the orders when the connection returns.

## 7. What we do NOT promise (protect trust)
- No offline mode until it has passed its test (Jan 2027 target). No feature is shown as live before it ships; the roadmap shows a month.
- No hardware resale margin.
- No card processing in 2026. We record card payments made on their existing terminal.
