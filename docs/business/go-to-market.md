# Go-to-Market: 20 Paying Clients by Dec 31, 2026

## 1. The calendar reality
- Today: **Sun Oct 4, 2026**. Deadline: **Thu Dec 31, 2026** = 12 weeks.
- A product to sell is ready around **week 5-6 (Nov 2-15)**.
- Restaurants will not switch systems in peak season. **Last installation ≈ Fri Dec 11.**
- So the real installation window is **~5.5 weeks**. Sales conversations must start **now**, before the product is finished (pre-sale + design partners).
- Signups after Dec 11 **pay now** (first month or annual) with a guaranteed installation on **Mon Jan 4, 2027**. They count as paying clients.

## 4. Channels (ranked by leverage)

1. **Warm leads (3-9) → design partners.** Free until Dec 1, then founder price. In exchange: weekly feedback, testimonials, and 2 referral introductions each.
2. **Accountants (contadores).** They handle SIN invoicing for small businesses and feel the deadline pain on behalf of many clients at once.
   - Offer: **20% recurring commission** for every paying client they refer, and/or a free accountant view (invoice export) for their clients.
   - Target: meet 10 accountants in October; activate 3 who each bring 2+ clients.
3. **Client referrals.** 1 free month for each referred client who pays. Ask at the moment of the "wow" (the first WhatsApp report).
4. **Walk-in visits by cluster.** Equipetrol, Av. San Martín, Monseñor Rivero, 2nd-4th rings, food courts. Visit **3-6pm** (off-hours). Bring a tablet with the live demo.
5. **Beverage distributors / suppliers.** Their sales reps visit every bar every week. Co-marketing: the distributor recommends us, and we give them a stock-alert integration later.
6. **Content (support channel, not primary).** Short Spanish videos for Instagram/TikTok/WhatsApp status: "Factura al SIN en 5 segundos", "El cierre de tu bar en tu WhatsApp", "Se cayó el internet y seguimos vendiendo".

## 5. Ideal client (who to say yes to first)
- Bar, resto-bar, or casual restaurant with **5-25 tables**, owner-operated.
- Currently on paper, Excel, or a basic old POS.
- Owner **not always on site** (the WhatsApp report matters most to them).
- In SIN groups 9-12 or already struggling with invoicing.
- Avoid in 2026: chains with 3+ locations, large hotels, and places that need card processing integration or delivery-app integration on day one.

## 6. Onboarding playbook (one visit, ~3 hours)
1. Before the visit: client sends their menu (photo/PDF) by WhatsApp. We load it.
2. Check their hardware against the supported kit. If missing, send the shopping list 3 days ahead.
3. On site: install the app, pair printers, create waiter PINs, run a test service with 5 orders.
4. Activate the WhatsApp report to the owner's number. Send the first one that same night.
5. Day 2 and day 7: check-in call. Day 25: "Are you staying?" conversation + referral ask.
