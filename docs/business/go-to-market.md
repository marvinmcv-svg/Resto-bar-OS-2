## 5. Channels (ranked by leverage)

1. **Warm leads (3-9) → design partners.** Free until Dec 1, then founder price, in exchange for feedback, testimonials, and 2 referral introductions each.
2. **Accountants (contadores).** They handle SIN invoicing for many small businesses at once.
   - Offer: **20% of each referred client's payments for their first 12 months**, plus a free accountant view (invoice export) for their clients.
   - Target: meet 10 accountants in October; activate 3 who each bring 2+ clients.
3. **Client referrals.** 1 free month for each referred client who stays past 30 days. Ask at the "wow" moment (the first WhatsApp report).
4. **Walk-in visits by cluster.** Equipetrol, Av. San Martín, Monseñor Rivero, 2nd-4th rings, food courts. Visit **3-6pm**. Bring the live demo on a phone and a portable printer if possible.
5. **Beverage distributors / suppliers.** Their reps visit every bar weekly. Co-marketing now; stock-alert integration later.
6. **Content (support channel).** Short Spanish videos: "El cierre de tu bar en tu WhatsApp", "Tu mesero no puede borrar ventas", "Factura sin volver a escribir".

## 6. Ideal client (who to say yes to first)
- Bar, resto-bar, or casual restaurant with **5-25 tables**, owner-operated.
- Currently on paper, Excel, or a basic old POS.
- Owner **not always on site** (the WhatsApp report matters most to them).
- Has (or will buy) one network thermal printer and has decent internet + a phone hotspot.
- Avoid in 2026: chains with 3+ locations, hotels, anyone who needs card-processing or delivery-app integration on day one, and places with no reliable internet.

## 7. Onboarding playbook (one visit, ~3 hours)
1. Before the visit: client sends their menu (photo/PDF) by WhatsApp; we load it. We send the hardware list with prices.
2. Payment of the first month (QR/transfer) when the date is confirmed. Our SIN invoice goes out the same day.
3. On site: install the cashier app, pair the printers, set up waiters' phones, create staff PINs and roles, and run a test service with 5 orders.
4. **Configure the backup internet** (router failover to a hotspot/4G modem) and print the paper order pad for emergencies.
5. Activate the WhatsApp report to the owner's number. Send the first one that same night.
6. Day 2 and day 7: check-in call. Day 25: "Are you staying?" conversation + referral ask (before the refund window closes).

## 8. Support
- **WhatsApp support 11:00-24:00, every day.** Published in the terms.
- Response target during service: 15 minutes. Outside hours: next day at 11:00.
- Every client has a 2-minute fallback: the printed paper order pad + backup internet.
- Device health dashboard (sync status, printer status, last seen) so we spot problems before the client calls.
